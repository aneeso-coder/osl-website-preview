Complaints Procedure

If we get something wrong, we want to know. Telling us will not affect the support you or the person you care about receives.

Draft scaffold

This page is a structural scaffold, not a published policy.

The headings below are the sections this document needs. The content of each has been left as [TO BE COMPLETED] because policy commitments for a CQC-registered provider must be written and approved by Obsidian Supported Living, with legal review where appropriate. Nothing on this page should be treated as a statement of what OSL currently does. This page must not go live in this state.

Our commitment

To be completed

[TO BE COMPLETED] State the commitment, including explicitly that complaining will not lead to any detriment. Connect it to the RICH value of honesty and candour.

This section must be drafted and signed off by Obsidian Supported Living with appropriate legal or regulatory review. It has deliberately been left empty rather than filled with generic wording — a regulated provider must not publish policy commitments it has not agreed.

How to complain

To be completed

What happens next

To be completed

[TO BE COMPLETED] Set out each stage with a timescale: acknowledgement, investigation, response, and the escalation route if the person remains unhappy. Timescales are commitments and must be agreed by the registered manager — none has been invented here.

If you are still not happy

To be completed

[TO BE COMPLETED] Give the escalation routes: the Local Government and Social Care Ombudsman (for local authority funded care), the funding local authority, and the Care Quality Commission. Confirm the CQC's role accurately — the CQC does not investigate individual complaints, and saying otherwise sets people up to be let down.

Support to make a complaint

To be completed

Easy read

Required

An easy read complaints procedure is essential.

A complaints procedure that the people it exists for cannot read does not function. An easy read version must be produced, co-produced with people OSL supports, and linked from here and from the easy read page. It should also be available on paper in every home.
