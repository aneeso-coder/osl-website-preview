Careers

Support work in Nottinghamshire, done properly.

You already know what bad support work looks like: no training, no backup, a rota that changes on a Sunday night. We are trying to be the other thing.

Apply to join us

Why work at Obsidian

Our RICH values — Respect, Integrity, Compassion and kindness, Honesty and candour — are written to apply to you as much as to the people we support.

What that means for you

Respect: your time and your expertise are not infinite resources.
Integrity: the job described at interview is the job you get.
Compassion: extended to you on your difficult days, not only expected from you.
Candour: raising a concern here is safe and expected, not career-limiting.

Who leads you

Our Registered Manager started as a support worker and has done nearly every role in the structure. Our Nominated Individual is an NHS consultant psychiatrist. You are not being managed by people who have never done this.

Training

Trained in-house, by an accredited trainer.

Our training is delivered internally rather than bought as an online tick-box. Our Registered Manager is an accredited trainer.

Personal safety interventions

Delivered by an accredited trainer. Focused on de-escalation and on reducing restrictive practice — not on holding people.

CPR and first aid

Including choking response and safe use of a defibrillator.

Moving and handling

Practical, in-person, and specific to the equipment in the homes you will work in.

Epilepsy awareness

Recognising seizures, responding safely, and knowing when it is an emergency.
