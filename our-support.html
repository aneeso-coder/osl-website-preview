Our support

Support built around one person at a time.

We provide personal care and support to adults across Nottinghamshire in their own homes. What that looks like depends entirely on the person — but how we approach it does not change.

Make a referral Talk to us

Supported living

The person has their own home, on their own tenancy, and we provide the support they need in it — up to 24 hours a day, seven days a week. The tenancy and the support stay separate, so a home never depends on who provides the care.

Supported living →

Learning disabilities and autism

Communication-led and autism-informed. Our Registered Manager holds a Postgraduate Certificate in Autism; our Nominated Individual is an NHS consultant psychiatrist and the parent of an adult with severe learning disability and autism.

Learning disabilities & autism →

Mental health

Recovery-focused support, scaled to what is genuinely needed at the time. Clinical understanding is built into how this service is led rather than bought in when something goes wrong.

Mental health →

Positive Behaviour Support

Behaviour that others find difficult is communication. We change the environment rather than the person, teach skills rather than manage incidents, and measure whether restriction is reducing.

Positive Behaviour Support →

Property solutions

The building is not a background detail. With our housing partner Beacon Supported Housing CIC we source and adapt homes around the person who will live in them, subscribing to the Real Tenancy Test and REACH Tenancy Standards.

Property solutions →
