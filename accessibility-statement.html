What this build already does

For the author of this statement

Accessibility measures built into this site, for you to verify and then describe accurately above.

  • One h1 per page, and heading levels that descend without skipping.
  • Descriptive alternative text on every image, including every placeholder.
  • All text meets or exceeds a 4.5:1 contrast ratio (3:1 for large text) in both the light and dark themes.
  • Visible keyboard focus indicators on every interactive element.
  • Full keyboard operation, including the navigation menus. A skip link is the first focusable element.
  • Body text at a minimum of 17px, and layouts that reflow to 400% zoom without horizontal scrolling.
  • Respects the operating system’s reduced-motion preference.
  • Semantic HTML landmarks, and form fields with real labels rather than placeholder text.
  • Light and dark themes, honouring the operating system preference with a manual override.
  • No third-party scripts, embeds, or network requests.
  • An easy read page.

These are design intentions, verified by hand during the build. They are not a substitute for an independent audit, and this statement must not claim conformance until that audit has happened.
