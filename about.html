About us

Obsidian Supported Living, Kimberley, Nottinghamshire.

A specialist supported living provider built by people who have done the job, understand the clinical picture, and have lived it at home.

Our story

Obsidian Care Ltd trades as Obsidian Supported Living. We are based at Bridge House in Kimberley, and we support adults across Nottinghamshire.

We are registered with the Care Quality Commission to provide personal care in supported living settings to adults with a learning disability and/or autism, and to adults living with mental health conditions.

Obsidian Supported Living was established out of one family’s experience.

Dr Bal Powar has worked in NHS psychiatry since 2000 and has been a consultant psychiatrist since 2011. He is also the parent of an adult who has a severe learning disability and autism. Obsidian was established on the basis of that experience — of knowing the system both as the clinician writing the plan and as the parent trying to get someone to listen.

Dawn Newby, our Registered Manager, came to it from the other direction: more than 25 years in social care, starting as a support worker alongside people with intellectual disabilities and working through nearly every role since.

That is what this organisation is built on — a consultant psychiatrist with a parent’s stake in getting it right, and a manager who has done the job at every level. It is why our RICH values are written to apply to our staff as much as to the people we support, and why we keep tenancy and support separate: so that a person’s home stays theirs, whatever happens to their support arrangements.

Meet the team

The people accountable for this service.

Registered Manager

Dawn Newby

More than 25 years in social care, from support worker to Registered Manager — Dawn has done nearly every job she now leads. She holds a Postgraduate Certificate in Autism, is an accredited trainer in personal safety, CPR and first aid, and her focus throughout has been helping people with complex needs live in their own community and lead a full life.

“I am developing a team who share my passion and want to provide high quality support, which enables people to have control over their lives.”

Nominated Individual

Dr Bal Powar

Obsidian was established out of Bal’s own experience as the parent of an adult with a severe learning disability and autism. An NHS consultant psychiatrist since 2011, with a distinction in Clinical Neuropsychiatry, his experience spans autism and neurodevelopmental care, mental health, and Care and Treatment Reviews across community, forensic and acute NHS services. He has sat on both sides of the table — the clinician writing the plan, and the parent trying to get someone to listen — and as Nominated Individual he is accountable to the CQC for how the service is run.

Our values

RICH — and it has to work both ways.

Plenty of providers publish values for the people they support. Ours apply equally to the people who work here. A support worker who is not respected will not reliably deliver respect.

R

Respect

For people we support: their home, their choices, their pace.
For our staff: their expertise, their time, their boundaries.

I

Integrity

For people we support: the support we described is the support that turns up.
For our staff: the job we described is the job you get.

C

Compassion and kindness

For people we support: patience when things are hard, not just when they are easy.
For our staff: the same, when your day has been hard.

H

Honesty and candour

For people we support and families: when something goes wrong, you hear it from us first.
For our staff: raising a concern is safe, and expected.
