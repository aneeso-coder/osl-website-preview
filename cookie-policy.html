Cookie Policy

What cookies and similar technologies this website uses, and how you can control them.

What cookies are

A cookie is a small text file that a website puts on your computer, phone or tablet when you visit. It lets the site remember things about your visit — for example which pages you looked at, or a setting you chose. Cookies cannot run programs, cannot carry viruses, and cannot install anything on your device. Some cookies last only while you are on the site (“session” cookies) and are deleted when you close your browser; others stay for a set period so the site recognises you when you come back (“persistent” cookies).

We describe cookies in four groups, following the categories the Information Commissioner’s Office uses:

  • Strictly necessary — needed for the website to work. These do not require your consent.
  • Functional — remember choices you make, such as light or dark display.
  • Analytics — help us understand how the site is used, so we can improve it.
  • Marketing — used to show or measure advertising. We do not use marketing cookies.

A note on how this site remembers your display choice

If you switch this website between its light and dark appearance, we remember that choice using a small item of browser local storage named osl-theme. Strictly speaking this is not a cookie, but we mention it for openness: it stays on your own device, it is never sent to us or shared with anyone, and it contains no personal information — only the word “light” or “dark”. You can clear it at any time through your browser settings.

Cookies this website uses

The pages of this site have been built to be lightweight and to set no tracking cookies of their own. However, the platform the site is hosted on, and any tools connected to it, may set cookies. The most common are:

  • Hosting and content platform (for example WordPress) — may set a small number of strictly necessary cookies to keep the site working and, for anyone logging in to edit it, to manage that login. [CONFIRM]
  • Google reCAPTCHA — if used on the contact or careers forms to prevent spam, this sets cookies from Google and is covered by Google’s own privacy policy and terms. [CONFIRM]
  • Analytics (for example Google Analytics) — if used, this sets analytics cookies that count visits and show which pages are popular. This data is not used to identify you personally. [CONFIRM]

How to control cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings — you can block all cookies, delete existing ones, or allow only certain sites. Every major browser explains how in its help pages, and aboutcookies.org gives step-by-step instructions. Please note that if you block strictly necessary cookies, some parts of the site — such as submitting a form — may not work.

Consent, and the law

Cookies are governed by the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR) alongside the UK GDPR, both regulated by the Information Commissioner’s Office. Under PECR, strictly necessary cookies can be set without consent, but any non-essential cookie — including analytics, reCAPTCHA and marketing — needs your consent before it is set.
