Who we are

  • We are called Obsidian Supported Living.

  • We are a team of people who help adults.

  • The government checks us. We are on the official list of care services.

What we do

  • We help you live in your own home.

  • We help people who have a learning disability. We help people who are autistic. We help people who find their feelings very hard.

  • You choose what you want to do. We help you do it.

Where we are

  • We help people in Nottinghamshire. Our office is in Kimberley, near Nottingham.

How to talk to us

  • You can phone us. Our number is 0115 718 1760.

  • You can email us. Our email is info@obsidiansupportedliving.co.uk.

If you are not happy

  • Tell us if you are not happy. It is okay to tell us.

  • You can tell a member of staff. Or you can phone us. Or you can ask someone you trust to tell us.
