Who we support

Adults with complex needs, living in their own homes

Obsidian Supported Living is registered to provide personal care to adults with a learning disability and/or autism, and to adults living with mental health conditions — both under and over 65.

Supported living is not a care home. The person holds their own tenancy, and we provide the support. Those two things stay separate, deliberately, so that a change in support never puts someone’s home at risk.

Our values

RICH

Four values. Each one applies to the people we support and to the people who work here — because a team that isn’t treated well cannot support anyone well.

R

Respect

For the people we support: their choices, their home, their routines. For our staff: their time, their expertise, their boundaries.

I

Integrity

For the people we support: we do what we said we would do. For our staff: we hold to what we promised at interview.

C

Compassion and kindness

For the people we support: patience on the hard days. For our staff: the same patience, on their hard days.

H

Honesty and candour

For the people we support and their families: when something goes wrong, we say so. For our staff: raising a concern is safe here.

Property

The right support is no use in the wrong house.

We work with our housing partner, Beacon Supported Housing CIC, to find and adapt homes that actually fit the person — not the other way round.

That has meant full refurbishments, a bespoke wet room, and a new two-bedroom wheelchair-accessible bungalow in Nottinghamshire. Tenancies are held to the Real Tenancy Test and the REACH Tenancy Standards.
