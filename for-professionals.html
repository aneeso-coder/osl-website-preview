The referral process

From first call to moving in.

The stages below are the structure of our process. The detail of each stage — including timescales — is being confirmed by the registered manager and is marked where it is not yet agreed.

  1. Get in touch

    Email referrals@obsidiansupportedliving.co.uk or call 0115 718 1760 and tell us about the person. We will tell you honestly and quickly whether we are likely to be the right service.

  2. Share the information

    We will need enough to assess properly — needs assessment, risk information, behaviour support plan and health information where they exist.

Our assessment approach

Assessment is not a form. It is working out whether we can actually deliver what this person needs, in a property that suits them, with a team we can recruit and keep.

Our Nominated Individual is an NHS consultant psychiatrist and our Registered Manager holds a Postgraduate Certificate in Autism — complex referrals are read by people who understand them clinically as well as operationally.

We would rather say no

Accepting a referral we cannot meet is how placements break down, and a breakdown costs the person far more than a delay. If we are not right, we will tell you at the earliest point we know.
